**StatBlock** — a large Archivo Black figure (tabular numerals) over a JetBrains Mono caps label. CAL's repeating unit for any metric: standings points, goals, viewer counts, KPIs.

```jsx
<StatBlock value="24" label="Matches" />
<StatBlock value="18–6" label="Record" accent="cyan" align="center" />
```

Keep labels to 1–2 words. Use `cyan`/`magenta` accents sparingly to flag a hero number.
